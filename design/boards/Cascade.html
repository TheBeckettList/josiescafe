An element (bacon, egg, avocado) never takes a dish off the menu. The dish shows what is missing and offers whatever you still have to swap in, from the green lights. A base (pancake mix, bagels, coconut bread, coffee beans) is the dish itself, so without it the dish goes out. Josie marks each ingredient as an element or a base once, in Inventory setup. Marking a dish out never touches ingredients.
