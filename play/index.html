Play Josie’s World

A pretend café you can run from your phone or laptop. Ring things up, make them at the bar, watch the kitchen, order as a guest. Nothing is real, and nothing leaves your device.

Open the play café
JosieOwner. Menu, prices, team, wages, pastries.
MayaManager. The day, the phones.
RosaRegister. Ring up, take payment, place sticks.
Beto & LupeBar and kitchen. Read tickets, set the lights.

Tap Play mode at the bottom left any time to switch who you are, open the guest website, or start over.

The first time takes a few seconds to set up. After that it opens fast and remembers where you left it.
